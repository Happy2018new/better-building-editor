# Interrupted Same-Alias Liquid Witness R11

The LOW epoch of the separately frozen opaque-cube experiment stopped during
the depth-1 mask query. Its original native result remains `completed=false`
and `restored=false`; seven cleanup operations failed because owner-bound
client verification returned no direct value. A fresh read-only query also
failed binding. No game mutation was retried after that failure.

Eight native LOW control PNGs were captured: four at depth 0 and four at
depth 1. The depth-0 case finished its native source/control checks; the
depth-1 case did not finish its pixel controls. Depth 3 and the HIGH,
coordinate and complement epochs were not run. The same-session independent
stone calibration passed and restored. The LOW empty-control image visibly
contains the remote black opaque cube, consistent with the installed constant
LOW alias. This observation cannot establish an atlas range or filter.

The separate read-only LOW audit uses the 256 predeclared cube samples per
face at independent camera FOV 66.1. Every sampled north pixel is RGB
`(12,12,12)` and every west pixel is `(9,9,9)` in all eight controls. Both
faces have control/depth delta RGB RMSE 0 and maximum absolute delta 0.
Minimum four-neighbor nominal boundary clearances are 3.922282 pixels north
and 16.604619 pixels west. This checks constant LOW stability only;
all-FOV geometry and distinguishing-label observations remain separate.
