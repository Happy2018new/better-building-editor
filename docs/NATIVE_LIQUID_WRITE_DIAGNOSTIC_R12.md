# R12 liquid probe publication fix and native visibility investigation

The V11 `projection_liquid=None` exception belongs to the diagnostic probe. It does not establish a product lifecycle failure. The new `tools/probe_liquid_original_sides.py` uses the safe `renderer` value from `probe_liquid_workspace.WORLD_STATE`, emits an empty group list while publication is pending, and preserves the original strict readiness predicate.

`bridge.project()` starts asynchronous `project_large()` work. `bridge.stop_projection()` intentionally releases that work and clears `projection_liquid`. `world_projection.py` publishes the liquid owner at its ready stage. The frozen V11 snapshot appended an unconditional `b.projection_liquid.renderer.active` read after the safe workspace snapshot, so its next full-projection poll could fail during normal preparation. V11 sources, expected values, images and result documents remain unchanged.

The new offline test executes the entire snapshot during inactive, pending and published states. Inactive and pending states produce no groups and do not pass `world_ready`; publication exposes the group and passes readiness. A real work error still raises. Two further tests verify that the diagnostic direct writer clears the previous cells, uses the exact front values, and rejects layered or unreserved cells before mutation. All three tests pass.

The native visibility investigation remains unresolved. V11 server and client snapshots agreed on water and support cells, but the native H/L images did not visibly contain them. The corresponding full projection was visible. That evidence does not prove that the product writer publishes a terrain mesh incorrectly.

The static candidate is the write path. `WorldAdapter.write()` uses `SetBlockByBlockPalette` for ordinary front-only stone/water; its `SetBlockNew` calls are reserved for tile rebuild stages. The authoritative ModSDK documentation says that `SetBlockByBlockPalette` sets palette blocks and returns generation success. `SetBlockNew` supports loaded terrain and exposes `updateNeighbors`; neither description promises a specific terrain render publication barrier. A palette-versus-direct comparison at the same cells and camera is required before changing the product adapter.

The frozen V12 plan makes that comparison narrowly:

- One H and one L built-in foreground-water source, each at the same NW view, captured through palette native, direct `SetBlockNew` native, and full projection.
- One empty native baseline, giving seven comparison images; one preflight and six stone-calibration images would make fourteen native PNGs in total.
- The direct writer accepts only reserved front-only stone/water cells with air background and no actor data. It uses legacy aux values and `updateNeighbors=False` while flow is frozen.
- Every native write and full projection requires at least two actual `GameRenderTickEvent` advances after its request. Server/client before-and-after snapshots, the frozen camera, raw capture bindings, and full pending-to-ready reads are retained.
- Normal, fixture cleanup and outer reserves remain 120/132/150 seconds from actual-world readiness, bounded by exact game creation plus 300 seconds. The five-active-game gate, one launch marker, exact owner/creation binding, ordinary stop and exact fallback remain in place.

The frozen-plan check and targeted QA passed on 2026-10-13. The final probe also records full-projection request counts and requires at least two actual render events before capture. Exactly one cold launch was subsequently authorized and dispatched. Its fresh capacity gate found zero active games and twenty exact unchanged residual rows.

The launch failed before actual-world readiness, fixtures or WGC. The primary startup receipt reports `Owned game violated the noactivation window contract`: the game became foreground during startup even though its window had the noactivate flag. The prior foreground was Edge, the observed foreground was the owned game, and the final foreground was Explorer; the receipt says `background_safe=False` and `foreground_unchanged=False`. No explicit activation, real input or resize command was issued. After startup stopped the game, the passive guard recorded `Owned game creation identity changed`; that is a secondary termination observation, not evidence of a product liquid failure.

Session `1a237d2368d44787a2497ccc50488703` belonged to owner `liquid-r12-native-write-v12`, with game PID 48412 / creation identity `134363368317217820` and MCDK PID 36652 / identity `134363368315048054`. Final verification finds both identities absent, the session stopped, the temporary project configuration removed, and frozen pack/helpers unchanged. Cleanup and persistence error lists are empty. Creation to final stopped verification took 22.169 seconds and met the original outer deadline. There are zero V12 native PNGs, and the single launch was not retried. The proposed native write comparison therefore remains unobserved.
